<h2 id="about">A new perspective on photography.</h2>

Gaussian splats recreate the appearance of a place from many photographs. Splativerse lets you explore these spatial photographs from inside an immersive scene on Meta Quest.

Create a compatible capture using a tool such as Scaniverse or Polycam, or obtain a scan from its creator. Transfer an SPZ, PLY or SPLAT file to your headset and import it through Splativerse's file picker. A small demonstration scene is included so you can try the controls before importing your own files.

## Explore, save, return.

Move through the scene with Touch controllers and save the viewpoints you want to revisit. Press **X** to open the current scan's saved Views, then point and pull the right trigger to return to a View. Press **B** to return to your library, where you can rename Views or choose a starting View.

Search and sort your scans, or hide a scan from the navigation list without deleting its file. The immersive controls can be moved lower or higher, or hidden entirely. Use the left trigger to bring them back.

## Your files, on your headset.

Splativerse works locally and does not require an app account. Your imported scans, settings and saved Views remain on your headset. This version does not provide public galleries, shared multiplayer spaces, cloud synchronization or companion-pack downloads. Read the [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for details.

The current Quest build is a development preview. Store listing preparation is underway; it is not yet available as an approved store release. Quest Pro has been used for testing. Large scans can run slowly, and broader device and performance validation is ongoing. Touch controllers are required; movement has no collision detection.

Visit [Arrival Space Splativerse](https://live.arrival.space/51106658_5465) to explore examples in a separate online platform, or [RadianceFields.com](https://radiancefields.com) to learn more about Gaussian splatting. Those services are separate from this viewer.
